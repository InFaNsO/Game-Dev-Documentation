## Elevator pitch

A purifier kept alive by a rite that leaks — it needs a constant feed of mani or she dies — dives into a sealed Accord facility that reportedly cracked **permanent immortality**, to steal it and finally stop feeding the rite. She duels the facility's engineered Husks one by one — read each wind-up, parry or dodge, strike back — and **tears the mani-gem out of each one**, ending a deathlessness centuries overdue. Every gem patches her armor and sharpens her blade, and pulls her one step toward what happened to *them*. **You master the blade as you lose yourself.**

## Setting & era

- **Mani world, post-Breach, BEFORE the Looter Shooter.** A dead, residue-soaked world of corrupted Husks and ruined Accord structures. Same art set, tone, and enemies that flow into the LS.
- **⚠ The location is specific (2026-08-11): the Accord's mani-medicine division** — a facility that was chasing a cure for death before the Breach, reverse-engineered Husk-deathlessness into a medicine after it, and turned **its own entire staff** into engineered Husks with a bad batch. It sealed itself as a tomb. **Every enemy in the game is its staff**, or one of the purifiers who came hunting the same prize and lost. A hospital built like a temple — which is why the ruin kit and enemy family read funerary. Full account: [[1k Last Rite - Lore Bible]] §3.
- **Four elemental strata — Bhu · Jal · Vayu · Agni — in ANY ORDER**, each granting a seal, all four opening the sealed sanctum where the heart waits; plus a tutorial before them. **Supersedes the old "~3 themed strata, re-skinned by the curse on every rebirth"** *(and, ⚠ **2026-08-12**, the fixed `Bhu → Jal → Vayu → Agni` sequence that replaced it — see [[1l Last Rite - World & Environment Bible]] §1.7)*.
- **The protagonist:** a purifier sheathed in the **Kavach Rite** — deathless, unstably, and a prisoner of her own cure. She comes to **steal**, not to lay the dead to rest; the mercy is real but it is a by-product ("take my pain" survives, reframed). **Supersedes "drawn ruin to ruin to lay the corrupted to rest."**

## The core hook — greed IS the loop

**⚠ REPLACED 2026-08-11. The old hook was "the curse IS the replay loop"** — claim the beast's heart, wake at the ruin's mouth, re-descend the same ruin ~5 times as a diegetic NG+. **That loop is cut** ([[1k Last Rite - Lore Bible]] §6). The dive is one-way, and the engine is the exchange rate instead:

- **Every gem is both the resource and the poison.** It patches the Shroud that keeps her alive; it is also made of exactly what hollowed everyone in this building. **The healing IS the disease** — and the player operates that trade knowingly, every fight.
- **Die mid-facility → respawn** = the normal combat retry; it needs no magic pickup to justify it, because the Rite already made her deathless before the game started.
- **Fraying buys power; scarring bills you forever.** Spending sanity is a real tactical option (damage up, resistance up); overspending permanently lowers the ceiling. **Greed is a loan with permanent interest.**

**The keystone marriage stands:** *the player's mastery rises as the protagonist's mind falls.* Getting good and coming apart are the same arc — the hook that sells a thin-story game.

## What changes each stratum (a re-learn, not bigger numbers) — all cheap

- **Palette shift = the depth/sanity HUD** — one LUT per stratum; the world's hue tells the player how deep they are, at a glance.
- **New tempos, not new stats** — each element owns a different rhythm, feint rate, and telegraph language. **New enemy = new read.**
- **The facility degrades as you descend** — deeper strata are more thoroughly failed, the staff more thoroughly wrong, the corruption shader hotter. One float, not new art.
- **A new lore layer** + the protagonist's own narration degrades — including the fallen purifiers who got this far and no further.

## Combat model — the reaction-layer *subset* of the locked design

Uses only what `Mechanics/Looter Shooter/06` locked for the reactive layer — **no spells / launcher / tactical grid** (those are Tactical Combat + later games):

- **Player kit (LOCKED 2026-06-10):** **Blade offense** (light strikes — proactive pressure + HP chip; a conscious *addition* to the original list, since "you master the blade" needs offense) · Parry — **Block** (wide window, small purge, chip) + **Perfect** (tight window, big purge, guaranteed **counter**, **ranged-deflect** sends projectiles back) · **Dodge** (i-frames for perilous attacks; safe, no purge — the greed-split) · **Purification finisher** (purge-break → mercy-kill). **≤2 unlockable techniques** — *expressive, not power*, unlocked **diegetically at stratum milestones**, never a skill tree (**mastery IS the progression**). Candidates (gray-box-open): perfect-dodge→counter; chain-parry stance. **⚠ Amended 2026-07-31, revised 2026-08-07 — the weapon's identity is decided (two transforming trick weapons; the player starts with the Rite Blade only); the elemental player layer (Mani Surge) is CUT from Game 1 scope, so both technique slots are unclaimed. See "D6 amendment — the player's weapons" below.**
- **Enemy attacks pose a "which response?" read:** parryable (deflect) · unparryable/perilous (must dodge) · ranged (Perfect-parry deflects it back) · **feints/mix-ups** (the core depth — punish premature parries) · **phases** (HP-threshold moveset shifts = the cheapest depth multiplier). Telegraphs are **colour/icon-coded and fairness is sacred** (Sekiro's perilous tell).
- **Resolution (LOCKED 2026-06-10): HP + a parry-fed purge meter** (Sekiro-style). Blade + counters chip HP; **Perfect parries fill purge fastest**; a broken purge meter staggers the guardian into the **purification finisher** ("take my pain" — the mercy-kill). **Parrying is the win condition, not just defense** → wires the purifier theme into the mechanics and **seeds the LS's forced-purification of named Husks.** Purge decays if you stop parrying. **⚠ Payoff amended 2026-07-15 — see "D5 amendment — the Purge ultimate" below (fill → player-triggered ultimate, not auto-finisher).**
- **Camera (LOCKED 2026-06-10): a single smart action-cam** that dynamically pushes into OTS/close framing on each telegraphed attack, then pulls back (Furi/Sekiro/E33 do this with ~one camera). **NOT the LS's two-mode dual-camera rig** — that switch only earns its keep with a grid to switch away from, so it **moves to Game 2 (Tactical Combat)**. Game 1's milestone = the **reaction layer + reaction-cam *feel*.** Multi-enemy stays **readable** (attacks resolve one at a time → the depth of mixed fights is the *planning between reads*, not parsing simultaneous chaos).
- **Movement model (LOCKED 2026-06-10):** Expedition 33-style **near-stationary timing duels** for v1 (cheapest, matches the reference, lowest-risk gray-box). Free-arena movement stays a possible *later* experiment, not v1.

## Replayability — four stacked layers

1. **Difficulty ascension** — the menu difficulty setting (D9) drives the defense-window delays. **⚠ 2026-08-11: the diegetic rebirth-ladder half of this layer is gone with the curse loop** ([[1k Last Rite - Lore Bible]] §6); escalation now lives in the stratum ordering and the menu tier.
2. **Per-duel ranking** — no-hit / time / style; the mastery chase.
3. **"The Rite" gauntlet** — all guardians back-to-back, one healthbar, for score (unlocks after first clear; near-free to build).
4. **★ The clean run as the replay engine (⚠ REPLACES "the mystery as the replay engine", 2026-08-11).** The old layer withheld the truth across rebirths and asked players to replay to *learn more*; the story is now **tragedy, not mystery** — the doom is visible from early on ([[1k Last Rite - Lore Bible]] §9), so there is no withheld payload left to sell a replay. What replaces it: **reaching the finale with sanity intact is the true victory, and a no-scar clean run is a genuine flex.** You replay to do it *cleanly* — and the ending fork (§"The heart & the choice") means a clean run also buys you the cheapest version of the final choice.
- **Future free update:** an **Endless Dungeon mode** once procgen matures to that level (a later P-tier) — remixed/procedural descents. v1 ships fully authored; the update lands when the tech exists (great for the Steam algorithm). **Stepping stone (LOCKED 2026-06-11): the Chaos Descent** — a post-ending randomized descent via random *selection* of authored content (no procgen); see "Elemental strata spine."
- **⚠ 2026-08-09 — PROMOTED: the Chaos Descent moves from v1-stretch to CORE v1**, as the endless pillar replayability now centers on (mixed per-encounter elements · **sanity as the run timer** · score = depth × sanity kept · unlocked at the first ending reached, true or Husk). The full-procgen Endless remains the eventual free update. The campaign itself gains an ending-screen score (kalpa count · deaths · score). See [[1j Last Rite - Shroud, Mani & Sanity]] §5.

## The heart & the choice

**⚠ REWRITTEN 2026-08-11 — supersedes "The reward & the ending"** (which had a *beast's heart* at the bottom acting as an immortality pickup, and a single unforked true ending). Full text: [[1k Last Rite - Lore Bible]] §8.

- **What's at the bottom:** the **lead researcher who first tested the immortality-medicine on themselves** — the one being who already won the prize she's chasing, and look what it cost. She is staring at her own future. **Not a beast guarding a heart-item.**
- **The truth is not withheld:** the immortality *is* the corruption that makes Husks, and the game says so early. It **rhymes with the Breach/Akashic** (callback, not plot-link) but the local horror is this facility's own — they built it on purpose.
- **The final fork:**
  - **Take it** *(the tragic true ending)* — deathless, mind-gone; she walks out to begin an endless wandering, handing the baton straight to the Looter Shooter. The becoming, **knowing**.
  - **Renounce it** — drop the gem-crutch, walk away, keep the leaking mortal rite she came in wanting to escape. Restraint: she leaves with exactly the problem she arrived with, and her mind.
  - **Husk out** *(failure, mid-dive)* — sanity zero arms it; the next death turns her where she stands. The same becoming, **blind**. Authored content, not a game-over screen ([[1j Last Rite - Shroud, Mani & Sanity]] §3.3).
- **How scarred she is by then decides how much that choice costs.**

## Sanity / madness — atmosphere, not unfairness

Mostly **atmospheric** (visual/audio distortion, degrading narration, hallucinated whispers) with only *fair* mechanical touches. **Fairness is sacred** — telegraphs stay readable always. *(Optional, later: a risk/reward "embrace the hallucination for power at a handicap" toggle.)*

> **⚠ AMENDED 2026-08-09 — sanity now has mechanical teeth; the firewall narrows to "combat information never lies."** Two pools mirroring the Shroud's layers: **fraying** (recoverable — sensory narrowing + HUD unreliability; diegetic reads always true; restored at shrines) and **scarring** (permanent per kalpa — every Nth death; the death-screen countdown; **zero arms the Husk ending**). Cue corruption is default-off forever — it exists only inside the "embrace the hallucination" toggle, tuned after playtesting. ### D5 amendment — the Purge ultimate (2026-07-15)

Supersedes D5's payoff chain (break → auto-stagger → finisher). The meter and its parry-fed identity stand; the payoff becomes **player-triggered**:

- **Fill:** parries only — **Block parry = 1×, Perfect parry = 2×** (reference values, playtest-open). Decays when idle (unchanged). Dodge still pays nothing — the greed split stands.
- **Full meter arms the Purge ultimate** (a player attack input; binding + animation TBD at gray-box). Using it **consumes the meter** and **purges** the guardian: **staggered — it forfeits its next attack turn** — and it **takes +25% damage while purged** (duration playtest-open).
- **Parrying remains the win condition:** the only road to the ultimate runs through parries.
- **The purification finisher is no longer purge-triggered** — it moves to the **killing blow** (the mercy-kill cinematic; "take my pain" fiction unchanged). The ultimate is the *mid-fight* purification act; the finisher is the last rite itself.
- **Reference-design note:** this is deliberately closer to Expedition 33's *primed* Break (fill the bar, then **choose when to cash it** with a triggering attack) than Sekiro's auto posture-break — the player authors the burst window. Combos amplify it: **enemy** attack-strings ([[1d Last Rite - Reaction & Feints Spec]] §6) make each hit its own parry read, so a fully-parried string is a fat meter payday.
- **Combo system (LOCKED 2026-07-15 — see [[1d Last Rite - Reaction & Feints Spec]] §6/§6b):** combos are **linked pre-made single-attack clips** chained via the Animator graph (cheap — a new combo ≈ one data asset), on two sides: **enemy chains** (authored signature spine + dynamic runtime recombination; difficulty = longer chains + tighter windows; the rebirth-remix multiplier) and a **player QTE-chained, AP-bounded combo** (Expedition 33-style forgiving timed-press offense — a *planning* layer, not twitch execution). **Economy split stays clean: AP = offense input · Purge = parry-fed defense payoff.** The player combo is a conscious offense expansion (reactive-half → two-sided); build the enemy-string core first (it's the M2 CinderScale dependency), the player AP-combo after. Full build breakdown in the **Combo System dev plan** (child of the Ship Plan's `m1-strings`).
- **D8 retarget:** the old "purge-to-finisher ~5–8 Perfects" reference now reads "meter **fill** ≈ 5–8 Perfects (or mixed Block/Perfect equivalent)."
- **Still open (gray-box):** +25% duration (through the stagger turn vs fixed ms) · whether the ultimate can whiff or is guaranteed on a staggerable target · per-guardian meters in mixed encounters (default: per-enemy, fed only by parries against that enemy) · meter carryover between duels (default: resets).

### D6 amendment — the player's weapons (2026-07-31, revised 2026-08-07)

Extends D6's locked "blade offense" lane with the weapon's identity. Nothing in D6 is reversed — the QTE/AP combo (D5 amendment), the ≤2-expressive-techniques rule, and the fairness firewall all stand.

> **⚠ CUT 2026-08-07 — the Mani Surge and the elemental player layer are removed from Game 1 scope.** The player gets **no Mani-based attacks at all**: no elemental absorption, no per-element signature attack, no elemental particle trails, and **no Mani meter**. Elements stay **enemy-side only**, which restores the original fairness firewall in its strictest form. **Purge remains the only player meter.** Technique slot #1 is free again — both of the ≤2 slots are now unclaimed.
>
> **⚠ 2026-08-09 — cut NARROWED, not reversed: mani returns player-side as *defense only*** — the **Shroud** economy (repair + elemental resist/weakness; one scalar reserve + an element tag). Everything offensive stays cut; **Purge remains the only *offensive* meter.** See [[1j Last Rite - Shroud, Mani & Sanity]] §2 and the 2026-08-09 log entries.

- **⚠ Weapon identity — AMENDED 2026-08-07: two transforming trick weapons, not one fixed blade.** The single one-handed blade is superseded. The player carries **two armaments, each with two forms** — Bloodborne's trick-weapon model, where variety comes from transformation rather than weapon count:
  - **The Rite Blade · *Samskara*** — the grip telescopes rearward and the sword becomes a naginata (Katana ↔ Halberd sets). The blade never changes; only the grip extends.
  - **The Pyre Censer · *Dhupa*** — a censer forged into a war hammer, its head sliding along a fixed-length haft to lock choked (one-handed) or extended (two-handed) (Club ↔ Maul sets).
  - **Transformation is a combat verb, not a menu action:** the transform-attack is a **chain step costing AP** that switches movesets mid-combo. It lives entirely on the player's turn, so it never competes with the enemy-phase parry read.
  - **Design law:** transformations must conserve mass and must never "bloom" — opening a shape reads as *lighter*. The mechanisms that work are split-laterally, telescope-the-haft, and slide-the-head; each weapon uses a different one.
